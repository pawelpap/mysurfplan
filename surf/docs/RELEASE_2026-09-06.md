All spot tuning is database-owned, schema-validated and versioned. A generic admin editor supports future worldwide spots, change notes, references, version conflicts, compatible-history restoration and a versioned default for future spots. Runtime rule selection contains no Bico or Bafureira presets and no hard-coded spot default.

Selected-time forecasts, hourly details and lesson conditions include offshore swell energy and sea-surface temperature. The energy card also shows approximate deep-water power when periods are available. Both metrics use the current chosen time or lesson start. They do not change the quality or required-experience calculation. Matching teal cards adapt to desktop and small phones.

## Interpretation limits

Energy is offshore energy density in kJ/m². The secondary kW/m power value uses forecast period as an approximation for spectral energy period and assumes deep water. Missing periods leave power unavailable; missing partitions are not silently treated as zero. High energy does not imply good or beginner-friendly surf.

Water temperature is a regional sea-surface forecast, not a beach measurement. Its roughly ten-day horizon is shorter than the 16-day wave outlook. Missing values remain unavailable; no stale temperature is extended into later days. Forecast refresh requests fetch marine, weather and temperature concurrently, with the existing cache/coalescing rules.
